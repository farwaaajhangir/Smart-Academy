Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find instant answers regarding Smart Academy admissions, scholarship thresholds, and payment policies.

Smart Academy offers merit-based scholarships automatically computed upon submitting your academic score:

  • 85% or higher: 25% Merit Scholarship
  • 70% to 84.9%: 15% Merit Scholarship
  • 50% to 69.9%: 5% Merit Scholarship
  • Below 50%: Standard admission fee (0% Merit Scholarship)

When you complete the Eligibility Checker, your selected course and computed merit scholarship percentage are transferred to the Fee Calculator section. You can then enter your full name in the Fee Calculator and click Calculate Fee & Review Breakdown to generate your Fee Challan.

Yes! Smart Academy allows stackable savings. You receive a 5% discount for 3-month enrollments or a 10% discount for 6+ month enrollments in addition to your earned Merit Scholarship.

After clicking Calculate Fee & Review Breakdown in the Fee Calculator, click Generate & Print Fee Challan / Voucher. An official multi-copy voucher will pop up ready to be saved as a PDF or printed.
